# Code of conduct

TemeCMS is built by people with different backgrounds, skills and first languages. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project meets, is expected to keep it a place where people want to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Accept a decision once it is made, even when you argued for another.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## What happens

Report a problem privately to **security@temecms.com**, the project's one private address. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may remove comments, lock a thread, or ask someone to change how they behave. For serious or repeated cases, someone may be banned from the project for a period or for good.

This code applies in every project space, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
