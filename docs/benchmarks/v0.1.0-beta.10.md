# Scale benchmark — v0.1.0-beta.10

> **Provisional (I-154).** Slice 1.10's release run, in a cloud container (4 vCPU, 15 GiB), never a quiet machine: freshly seeded with a million items, 40,000 comments, 100,000 redirects, a 404 log of 100,000 addresses and 10 million AI requests (`teme:bench:seed --items=1000000 --comments=40000 --redirects=100000 --not-found=100000 --ai-activity=10000000`, 2,944 s, the database 17 GB on disk), vacuumed, then run with the load average at 0.79, 1.33, 1.39 and the CPU 95% idle. It may be run again locally before the tag.
>
> **What changed since beta.9.** Three media scenarios have new names: "media: images, deep page", "media: video" and "media: one month", after the tabs that went. Against beta.9, the statements that moved: the categories screen's page one 2 → 3; the tags screen's page one and deep page 1 → 3 (probably 1.10p7's live tab counts and chosen terms); `editor.open` and `editor.open_with_autosave` 14 → 15 (probably the featured image's credit and licence, one statement, 1.10p8); the front page, a category, an author's page one and page 400, the year archive and the four searches 1 more each (16 → 17 and 17 → 18 for the searches; the cause was not traced); `public: article` 29 → 28. Every other scenario's statement count is as in beta.9.
>
> **The AI record among 10 million rows.** Page one: 10.9 ms, 4 statements. Halfway down (5,000,000 rows): 10.2 ms, the same statements. A month's filter (2026-04, 759,493 rows): 11.8 ms on page one and 37.7 ms halfway in, 4 statements each. A person's month: 13.3 ms, read by `ai_activity_user_index`. A task's month (alt text): 9.7 ms, read by `ai_activity_task_index`. The spend and the filters: 20.1 ms, from the monthly totals (it reads no `ai_activity` index). No scenario scanned `ai_activity` in sequence.
>
> **Handed over by the sweep.** The terms screen's tabs' counts: 24.8 ms, 1 statement, within its 100 ms budget; the plan names `terms_taxonomy_parent_id_index` and no sequential scan of `terms` was reported. The media months: 2.5 ms, 1 statement, over 1,000,000 files, from `media_live_created`.
>
> **What was fixed.** Nothing needed fixing.
>
> **What failed and wasn't fixed.** 22 scenarios are marked FAIL, 15 of which failed the same way in beta.9: the same time budgets and the same sequential scans (comments, redirects, the sitemap's contents and terms), none on `ai_activity` and none in a table this slice added. Seven are new, all by time alone with the plans unchanged, on a container that seeded 1.5 times slower than beta.9's and ran every public page 1.3 to 1.8 times slower: `category filter, with children` 252.4 ms (budget 200), `public: article` 120.0 (100), `public: article, 20 tags` 133.5 (100), `public: comments, page 2` 168.8 (150), `cache: miss, article` 120.1 (100), `cache: wait, author page` 17.3 (10), `cache: miss, article (Redis)` 104.7 (100). Under I-153 and I-154 these are the container's, recorded for a local run, not a defect of slice 1.10.
