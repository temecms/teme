# Scale benchmark — v0.1.0-rc.12

> **Provisional (I-154).** Slice 1.12's release run, in a cloud container (4 vCPU, 15 GiB), never a quiet machine: freshly seeded with **360,000 items, not a million**, 40,000 comments, 100,000 redirects, a 404 log of 100,000 addresses and 10 million AI requests (`teme:bench:seed --items=360000`, 677 s, the database 9.6 GB on disk), vacuumed, then run with the load average at 1.61, 1.30, 1.45 and the CPU 95.2% idle. The container has about 20 GB of free disk: a seed of a million items filled 7 GB in its first ten minutes and would have passed what was free (rc.11's made 17 GB), so it was stopped; 200,000 items ran the scenarios up to an author page, `/author/author-2/page/84/`, that only a bigger site has, and stopped there with a 404; 360,000 is the most that fitted and still has that page. The backups' own run is `docs/benchmarks/v0.1.0-rc.12-backup.md`.
>
> **What changed since rc.11.** Slice 1.12 adds no scenario. Against rc.11 the lists’ and the public pages’ statements did not move, apart from these: `title search: lighthouse 5` 7 → 2 queries; `quick edit, nothing waiting` 20 → 19 and `quick edit, draft waiting` 18 → 17 queries; `cache: miss, article` and `cache: miss, article, static driver` 39 → 35 queries; `cache: quick edit, with warming` 30 → 31 and `cache: quick edit, static driver` 31 → 30; `scheduler.claim` writes 1,101 → 1,104; and **`bulk.edit, per item (batch of 25)` 418 → 494 queries and 25 → 232 writes** (a move to look at, though it passes). The deepest-page scenarios follow the site’s size: `public: author, page 400` is now page 150 and `public: sitemap, posts page 316` is page 114. The five Redis rows are not measured: no Valkey answered at 127.0.0.1:6379 (the container’s runs on 6380).
>
> **What failed.** 12 scenarios are marked FAIL, none fixed (I-3169). Ten failed in rc.11 too: `scheduler.claim`, `public: article, 20 tags`, `public: article, long thread`, `comments: what Empty spam covers`, `comments: delete permanently, per comment`, `comments: erase addresses, the daily run`, `redirects: halfway down`, `redirects: the tabs’ counts`, `public: sitemap index, its pages found afresh` and `public: sitemap, posts page one`. **Two are new:** `public: sitemap, posts page 2` (128.8 ms, 3 queries, within its 150 ms budget) and `public: sitemap, posts page 114` (36.9 ms, within its 200 ms), both marked FAIL because their plans read `contents_pkey` and not `contents_sitemap`: at 360,000 items the planner chooses the primary key where a million items made it choose the sitemap index. The four search scenarios that failed rc.11 by time alone pass here.
