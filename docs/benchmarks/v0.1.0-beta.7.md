# Scale benchmark — v0.1.0-beta.7

> **Provisional (I-154).** Slice 1.7's release run, on the shared development machine. The million-article data set of 1.6h (B-216), brought forward in place for 1.7's six page-cache migrations (`migrate --database=bench`, each a small table) and vacuumed (26 s); it was kept (no reseed). The host settled from 43 to 58% average CPU in the minutes after it woke from sleep to 21.6% in the minute before the run.
>
> **Two changes in method, and what they did to the uncached pages.** Every public request starts in a fresh scope (D5), as a production request does, so each reads what the cache store keeps for the settings, where the run's earlier requests used to leave it in the scope: four statements on every public page (an article drawn in a fresh scope runs 23, in a kept one 19). And issue 30's fix (Task 1): a listing reads its authors' addresses in one statement and its cards' images in one, and the front page reads its cards as one list. Against beta.6: the front page 38 → 16; an article 25 → 23; the twenty-tag article 23 → 24; a category 25 → 17; an author's page one 25 → 17, and page 400 25 → 17; the year archive 30 → 15; search, a common word 28 → 15, two words 31 → 15, mid-frequency words 29 → 15, a rare word 10 → 14 (few cards, so D5's four reads show); `public.item` 19 → 17. The admin's scenarios keep their one scope and compare with beta.6's; `editor.open` and `editor.open_with_autosave` count one fewer (15 → 14), since `CardMedia` remembers the item's media across their runs.
>
> **Check 4's pair still fails, the plan's fault (B-344):** `public: article, 20 tags` ran 24 statements to the random `public: article`'s 23. After issue 30 an article's count is fixed by what it shows, never by how many cards it has: 23 with no image; a featured image adds its caption and its image (+2); any card image adds one statement for all the cards (+1). The twenty-tag article is 23 plus its related cards' images; the random one showed none.
>
> **The page cache.** A hit reads nothing from the database on either store (check 5): the file store 3.0 ms for an article, 4.2 for the front page, 1.9 for a search, 1.5 for the twenty-tag article with a CDN; Redis 3.6 ms and one `EVAL`; all within `public-hit`'s 10 ms. A miss costs, over the same article drawn uncached: the file store 5 SELECTs (the latest purge's number, the next clock change, the race guard's look before and after the write, the folder's name read back) and 4 writes (the lock taken and freed, the page's rows cleared and written); the static-file driver 5 and 5 (and the file's row); Redis 4 SELECTs, no write and 5 commands. The misses' times, an article's 298.1 ms against its uncached 181.7, the front page's 241.1 against 69.7, the static driver's 181.1 and Redis's 141.7, are over their budgets on this host (B-346). A `STALE` answer: 13.9 ms on the file store, no SELECT and 2 writes (the refused lock, B-332); 4.9 ms on Redis, 3 commands. A wait: 96.2 ms on the file store, whose timing includes the other request's storing of the page, 26 to 134 ms here where D9 expected a fraction of a millisecond (B-345); 6.7 ms on Redis, 5 commands. A change: `cache: quick edit`, 29 SELECTs and 7 writes against `quick edit, nothing waiting`'s 20 and 3, for its footprints before and after, the purge's number, the post's page removed and the front page set aside, and one no-op `DELETE` (#54); with a CDN, 1 SELECT and 3 writes more (the drain's check; the CDN's queue insert, and the drain's unique lock refused, its `INSERT` and its `UPDATE`); with warming, the store's two reads for what to draw again and the `WarmPages` row; with the static-file driver, 2 SELECTs more (the files' rows of the page removed and of the page set aside); on Redis, 24 SELECTs, 4 writes and 9 commands. The static-file driver's prune on an idle site: 12.2 ms, 3 SELECTs and 2 writes (forgetting old purges, and #54's no-op). The tags a page carries: the twenty-tag article 56, 1,790 characters written out, all of them in `X-Teme-Cache-Tags` (under its 2,000); the front page 51 (1,310); a common word's search 47 (1,318); an article 21 (602).
>
> **Four counts differed from the plan's fixed table, the plan's fault (B-343),** each measured on `teme_bench`. (a) and (b): the static driver's and Redis's article misses ran 31 and 30 SELECTs where the table said 28 and 27, give or take 1. On the same article, measured on eight random articles, each store adds exactly its cost: the file store 5 SELECTs and 4 writes, the static driver 5 and 5, Redis 4, no write and 5 commands. The articles themselves run 23, 24 or 26, and these two runs drew 26s. (c): the file store's wait sent 3 writes where the table said 2: Laravel's lock lottery, `delete from cache_locks where expiration <= ?`, came in 1 run in 30 (2 in 100 by default). (d): the quick edit with warming ran 30 SELECTs where the table said 31: a quick edit runs 28 or 29 by whether its post has a featured image, whose media it checks, and warming adds exactly two `PageStore::keys()` reads and one jobs row; this run's warming post had none.
