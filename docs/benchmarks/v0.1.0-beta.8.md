# Scale benchmark — v0.1.0-beta.8

> **Provisional (I-154).** Slice 1.8's release run, in a cloud container (4 vCPU, 15.7 GiB), never a quiet machine: freshly seeded with a million items and 40,000 comments (`teme:bench:seed --items=1000000 --comments=40000`, 1,440 s), vacuumed, then run with the load average at 1.60, 1.44, 1.64 and the CPU 97.6% idle. It may be run again locally before the tag. The first attempt stopped at "public: search, rare word", answered 500: search's rate limit uses the `database` cache store on the default connection, and with `DB_PORT=5433` that database, `teme`, didn't exist in the container; it was created and migrated, and the run repeated from the start.
>
> **What changed in the data since beta.7.** Posts are seeded Open and pages Closed, as their types start; beta.7's were all Closed, the column's default, so every article now draws the comment form and reads its counts. The posts with comments (*n* % 100 = 17) are left out of "public: article". Against beta.7, the public pages' statements: the front page 16 → 16; an article 23 → 29; the twenty-tag article 24 → 27; a category 17 → 17; an author's page one and page 400 17 → 17; the year archive 15 → 15; the four searches 14, 15, 15, 15, unchanged; `public.item` 17 → 20; `cache: miss, article` 28 → 32. Only the pages that draw one article moved, by what the open thread and its form read. Check 4's pair still fails, B-344's fault: the random article ran 29 to the twenty-tag article's 27, two apart, as a featured image adds two.
>
> **The comments.** Every comment scenario is within its budget. The long thread's article: 93.1 ms of `public-item`'s 100, 29 statements, the same as an article without comments, reading `comments_conversations` and `comments_thread`. Its page 2: 77.0 ms; page 80: 57.7 ms, 16 statements each, so the deep page costs what page 2 costs (check 4), both from `comments_conversations` and `comments_thread`. The link to its last comment, the deepest reply of the last chain: 8.7 ms, 8 statements, a 302 to its page. The Pending view: 4.6 ms on page one and 4.0 halfway down, 2 statements each, from `comments_waiting`. The views' counts: 18.6 ms, 2 statements, Spam and Trash from `comments_parked`. The badges: 5.4 ms for an editor and 4.0 for an author, one statement each; neither read `comment_stats_waiting`, since `comment_stats` holds about a thousand rows here and the planner reads it whole, below the rule's 10,000. The trash purge's chunk: 5.7 ms from `comments_parked`. One Bulk Edit Discussion step: 5.5 ms an item.
>
> **What failed in the comments, reported and not fixed here.** (1) *What Empty spam covers* (25.7 ms of 200): a sequential scan on `comments`. Spam is 8,000 of the 40,000 comments, a fifth of the table, so the planner reads the table rather than `comments_parked`. (2) *The erasure's daily run* (62.1 ms of 500): it read `comments_addresses` for the comments to erase, and a sequential scan on `comments` for the outer `UPDATE … WHERE id IN (…)`, joined by hash: at 40,000 rows the planner prices a scan of the table below a thousand primary-key lookups. Both are the planner's choice on a table that size; the rule flags any table over 10,000 rows. (3) *One deletion inside Empty spam*: 9.1 ms a comment, so 1,000, what an Empty press deletes in its own request, take 9.1 s, inside 10; and 5,000, one queued part, 45.3 s, over half the worker's 60. `IN_REQUEST` and `PER_JOB` are 1.8c′'s decision, a finding to review.
>
> **Times over budget with clean plans** (the four admin searches, 344 to 600 ms; `scheduler.claim`, 1,712.6 ms) were over in beta.7 too, and stay provisional under I-153 and I-154, recorded under B-218's rule (B-346).
