# Scale benchmark — v0.1.0-rc.11

> **Provisional (I-154).** Slice 1.11's release run, in a cloud container (4 vCPU, 15 GiB), never a quiet machine: freshly seeded with a million items, 40,000 comments, 100,000 redirects, a 404 log of 100,000 addresses and 10 million AI requests (`teme:bench:seed --items=1000000 --comments=40000 --redirects=100000 --not-found=100000 --ai-activity=10000000`, 1,897 s, the database 17 GB on disk), vacuumed, then run with the load average at 0.85, 1.08, 1.33 and the CPU 100% idle. A first run, before a Valkey was started in the container, measured the same scenarios without the five Redis rows; this is the second. The import's own run is `docs/benchmarks/v0.1.0-rc.11-import.md`.
>
> **What changed since beta.10.** Every tenth file is uploaded for an item, and "media: uploaded for one item" is new: 4.6 ms, 2 statements, within its 200 ms budget. Against beta.10, no list's statements moved. An article page's moved by one: `public.item` 19 → 20, `public: article, every fallback in play` 31 → 32, `public: article, long thread` 31 → 32, `cache: miss, article (Redis)` 32 → 33 and `cache: miss, article, static driver` 38 → 39 (I-1773: `CardNodes` reads the shown revision's document a second time once any card gives structured data). `public: article` (28 → 34 here, 30 in the first run), `public: article, 20 tags` (29 → 30) and `cache: miss, article` (33 → 39) moved by more because these scenarios read articles picked at random, so their counts follow the pick; the extra statements were not traced to a cause. With the plain article at 34, the rule that an article with 20 tags may not run more statements than it fails `public: article, 20 tags` (30 queries, but `public: article` ran 34), which it passed in the first run (30 and 30).
>
> **The address check at 100,000 items** (I-913): 21.84 ms an address over 2,000 addresses; 100,000 addresses would take about 36 minutes (`CheckCostTest`, 195 s; 1.11m measured 20.75 ms at 10,000 items, so the cost does not grow with the site).
>
> **What was fixed.** Nothing needed fixing.
>
> **What failed and wasn't fixed.** 13 scenarios are marked FAIL, down from beta.10's 22, and none is new: all 13 failed in beta.10 too. Six fail by time alone with their plans unchanged, on a container whose CPU and disk are not a quiet machine's (I-153, I-154): `media search: harbour` 500.0 ms (budget 300), `picker search: river` 518.3 (300), `search.body_rare_word` 626.1 (300), `palette.mixed` 356.6 (300), `public: article, long thread` 122.0 (100), `comments: delete permanently, per comment (Empty spam, 25 a run)` (1,000 items would take 11.4 s, over 10 s). Five carry the sequential scans slice 1.9 and slice 1.10 recorded and left, none on a table slice 1.11 added: `comments: what Empty spam covers` and `comments: erase addresses, the daily run` (comments, 40,000 rows), `redirects: the tabs' counts` (redirects, 100,000 rows), `public: sitemap index, its pages found afresh` (contents, 995,097 rows, and terms, 52,000) and `public: sitemap, posts page one` 152.9 ms (the same two). Two fail a statement-count rule: `redirects: halfway down` runs 7 statements where page one runs 4, and `public: article, 20 tags`, above. They are left to decide whether to fix before the tag.
