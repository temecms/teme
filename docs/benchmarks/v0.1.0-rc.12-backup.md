## What the runs say

- **Back up now** took 0.1 s from the request to its first step, without the scheduler’s minute: on a server the scheduler takes a waiting backup within a minute, once no backup, prune or check holds the backup lock (I-3172).
- **What the place holds:** 2.8 GB, counted by `restic stats --mode raw-data` in 5.1 s; Tools › Backups waits up to 60 s for it (I-2986).
- **The manifest in memory:** the first backup’s peak memory, 154.5 MB, holds every original’s SHA-256 (I-2481).
- **Spare disk:** a backup streams the database’s dump into restic and needs no room for it. The weekly test restore needs room for the dump, 149.7 MB, and a spare database the size of the site’s, about 2.9 GB. A restore needs room for the dump and the media, 2.6 GB.
- **At full size:** the first backup’s media step took 26.8 s for 2.6 GB; at full size, about 26.3 GB, it would take about 268.0 s at this machine’s rate. A place off the server adds the time its link takes to carry that much.

## On disk

- The place: 2.9 GB on disk, for 40,000 images and the backup the prune kept; it forgot 1 backup.
- restic’s cache: 76.9 MB after the runs; 76.5 MB on the new server after the restore.
- Free disk: 19.8 GB before the runs, 7.8 GB after them.

## The export

`php artisan teme:export` on this site: 53.7 s, peak memory 54.5 MB, its folder 1.7 GB (39,640 originals copied).
