## You may, without asking

- **Say what you use or build on:** "built with TemeCMS", "runs on Teme", "a theme for TemeCMS", "a plugin for Teme".
- **Name your plugin, theme or service "for TemeCMS" or "for Teme"**, such as "Galleries for TemeCMS" or "Hosting for Teme", as long as your own name comes first and it is clear the project doesn't make it.
- **Write about TemeCMS** in articles, tutorials, books, talks and videos, and use the logo to illustrate them, unchanged.
- **Link to the project** with the name or the unchanged logo.
- **Run unchanged copies** of TemeCMS under its own name, for yourself or for others.

## Please don't

- **Ship a changed version under the TemeCMS or Teme name.** A fork is welcome under its own name. Say it is "based on TemeCMS" if you like, but give it a different name and logo.
- **Use the names or the logo so that your product, company, domain or account looks official**, endorsed or made by the project, such as "TemeCMS Pro", "Official Teme Themes", or a domain like `temecms-download.com`.
- **Change the logo** (its shape, colours or proportions), or combine it with your own mark.
- **Use the names or the logo for something unrelated** to TemeCMS.

## Asking

For anything this page doesn't cover, or to ask for permission, write to **security@temecms.com** with "Trademark" in the subject. We answer every request.
